## What Validity proved

Validity proved that, under a stress-state assignment in which multiple coverage tests fail simultaneously and available Interest Proceeds are insufficient to satisfy the aggregate Debt Payment Sequence draw requirements, the waterfall obligations triggered by clauses § 11.1.1.1(F), (H), (K), and (M) cannot all be satisfied from the available proceeds pool.

The relevant limitation is supplied by § 11.1.2, which directs disbursements to be made in waterfall order only to the extent funds are available.

The solver returned CONDITIONAL. The resource-exhaustion condition is not universal. It is proven under the specific assignment described below.

---

## Stress-state assignment

The finding holds where:

Available Interest Proceeds < aggregate Debt Payment Sequence draws required to restore all simultaneously breached coverage tests to pro forma compliance.

In that state, the triggered draw obligations compete for the same finite proceeds pool. The waterfall remains operative, but not all triggered obligations can be fully satisfied.
